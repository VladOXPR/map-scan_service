# Privacy Policy — CUUB

_Draft for hosting at `https://cuub.tech/privacy` (or your chosen https URL). Have counsel review before App Store submission._

**Last updated:** 2026-11-02

CUUB (“we”, “us”) provides a mobile application that helps you find nearby battery-sharing stations.

## Information we collect

- **Phone number** — to send a one-time SMS code and create your account.
- **Precise location** (when you allow it) — to show nearby stations and for on-device low-battery proximity alerts. Each time you open the app or come back to it, each time you tap Find nearest station, and each time you unlock a power bank, we also record where you are (to about 10 meters) together with your app session and, if you are signed in, your account. Apart from return alerts (below), we never record your location in the background or follow you continuously.
- **Location while you have a power bank** (only if you turn on return alerts) — while a power bank you unlocked in the app is out, the app checks where your phone is about every 150 meters, including when the app is in the background, for at most 24 hours after you unlock it, and compares it with the public station map on your phone to alert you when you pass a station with an open slot. These locations stay on your phone: they are not sent to us or recorded. It stops when you return the power bank, 24 hours after you unlocked it, when you turn return alerts off (tap the rental timer at the top of the map), or when you sign out. While it runs, iPhone shows the location indicator in the status bar and Android shows a notification.
- **Phone battery** — your battery level, whether the phone is charging, and whether Low Power Mode is on, recorded with your app sessions, the actions you take in the app, and location records.
- **App usage** — the screens you view, buttons you tap, how long you use the app, links and notifications you open, and crashes, with your app version, operating system, language, time zone, network type and an approximate area (about 1 km). Usage is recorded under an install identifier that can survive reinstalling the app, and is linked to your account once you sign in.
- **Session recordings** — our analytics provider may record how you move through the app's screens to help us find problems. Text you type is masked.
- **Account identifiers** — a user id and session token stored securely on your device.
- **Device notification permission** — used for local (on-device) low-battery and return alerts. Remote push tokens are only collected if that feature is enabled later.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## How we use information

- Authenticate you and keep you signed in.
- Show map pins for CUUB stations.
- With return alerts on, tell you when you pass a station with an open slot while you have a power bank.
- Send SMS verification codes via our service provider (Twilio).
- Understand demand: where and when people look for a charge or a place to return a power bank, whether a station with batteries or an open slot was nearby, and how long it took to get a power bank and to return it. We use this to decide where to add stations and when to restock them.
- Operate and improve the service, and respond to support requests.

## Sharing

We share data with service providers that process it on our behalf (for example SMS delivery, cloud hosting, and product analytics through PostHog), subject to appropriate agreements. Location records stay with us and only an approximate area (about 1 km) goes to our analytics provider, though a session recording can show the map around you. We may disclose information if required by law.

## Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). Deletion removes or irreversibly deactivates your app user record. Location records from your account (including any recorded by return alerts in earlier versions of the app), and from your phone while you were signed out, are separated from you and from your rentals and rounded to about 100 meters; we keep them only as anonymous counts of demand by area. You may also contact support via the Support URL in the app.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke location and notification permissions in system settings. Without location permission we record no location.
- Turn return alerts on or off at any time by tapping the rental timer at the top of the map. Renting works the same either way.
- Log out or delete your account in the app.
