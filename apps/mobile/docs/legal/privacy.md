# Privacy Policy — CUUB

_Draft for hosting at `https://cuub.tech/privacy` (or your chosen https URL). Have counsel review before App Store submission._

**Last updated:** 2026-08-06

CUUB (“we”, “us”) provides a mobile application that helps you find nearby battery-sharing stations.

## Information we collect

- **Phone number** — to send a one-time SMS code and create your account.
- **Precise location** (when you allow it) — to show nearby stations and for on-device low-battery proximity alerts.
- **Account identifiers** — a user id and session token stored securely on your device.
- **Device notification permission** — used for local (on-device) low-battery alerts. Remote push tokens are only collected if that feature is enabled later.

We do not sell your personal information.

## How we use information

- Authenticate you and keep you signed in.
- Show map pins for CUUB stations.
- Send SMS verification codes via our service provider (Twilio).
- Operate and improve the service, and respond to support requests.

## Sharing

We share data with service providers that process it on our behalf (for example SMS delivery and cloud hosting), subject to appropriate agreements. We may disclose information if required by law.

## Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account). Deletion removes or irreversibly deactivates your app user record. You may also contact support via the Support URL in the app.

## Your choices

- Deny or revoke location and notification permissions in system settings.
- Log out or delete your account in the app.

## Contact

See [Support](./support.md) or email the address published on your Support page.

## Children

CUUB is not directed at children under 13.
